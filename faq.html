FAQ

Why ProtocolLib?

For the latest BedrockPOV UserLines packet handling without making the real player carry server-side passengers.

Why avoid passengers?

Passenger-based UserLines can interfere with cross-world teleport systems such as Multiverse.

What is BedrockPOV?

A separate UserLines configuration controlling what Bedrock viewers see, including text, enabled state, SelfVisible and HeightOffset.

How do I show 100% ❤?

PlayerHealth:
  enabled: true
  HealthStructure: "PercenHeart"

Does GTabs replace vanilla signal bars?

No. The custom numerical TAB ping feature was removed; vanilla connection bars remain untouched.

Why no ms in %player_ping%?

It returns only the colored number. Append ms yourself if wanted.

How do I apply changes?

Use /gtabsreload for YAML changes. Use a full restart for JAR or dependency changes.
